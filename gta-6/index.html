  2. 2

    Set up Jason's house

    SetupJasonMedium priority

    Strength (max health): lift the heaviest weights on the back deck, resting between reps until the game says you need to recover; no gym needed. Cardio (max stamina): hang the punching bag from the storage room / shed and punch until the session ends. Focus (slow-motion time): shoot hoops in the driveway or feed Dolores the snake. Have some protein after a workout: it speeds recovery so you can train twice a day (Caspahz). Then sleep in your bed to skip to the next day and train again; the stats video says this maxes Strength and Cardio in a couple of hours. Read the Fishing Magazine and check the mailbox for cash. Repairing the house raises your relationship with Lucia; leaving the walls unpainted lowers it a little. Jay Dunna's Chapter 1 walkthrough: repair the house before Lucia comes home (walls, the deck by the door, the stairs, the leaky roof via the ladder, then paint), and hang the hidden punching bag for a home workout spot she likes. Feed Dolores, and do Lucia's shopping, flowers and clothes from Andy before picking her up.

    Reward / unlocks: Stats that carry through the game (Jason's Strength and Cardio reset at the end of Chapter 1)

  4. 4

    Get around faster: grab a helicopter

    SetupJasonMedium priority

    There is no real fast travel. A pontoon helicopter is docked at a small building in Pelican Valley (closest to Jason's house; the NPCs there shoot a little) and another floats at an oil/gas platform further north; the Southwind airfield has one too. Land it in the water by Jason's dock and drop the anchor from the vehicle menu. It cannot be refuelled, so treat it as a burner, and land away from enemy camps. Also useful: saving and immediately loading that save puts you back at home, and holding down on the D-pad then the cinematic button lets the car or boat auto-drive to your waypoint. Once you have the RideMe app, request a ride, hold aim on the driver and hold Triangle / Y to skip the trip for $100. 'H' markers on the map are helicopter spawns, but they come and go with the time of day.

  5. 5

    Gear Up

    StoryJasonHigh priority

    Unlocks gun shops and loadouts. Buy a suppressor and a body armor vest (it breaks if fully depleted, but re-equipping it repairs it). Pick up a bandana, hat and sunglasses and two or three outfits: toggle the accessories off from the item wheel when the cops have your description, and save outfits to your car to swap during a chase. Weapon colours: blue = bought (kept forever, moddable), yellow = unique (kept forever, fixed mods), white = found (lost if you die or are busted; the trunk keeps one per weapon type). Away from the car you carry two long guns. Sheriff cars carry a free armor vest and guns in the trunk. If the cops saw your weapon, stash it in your trunk; if they know your face, hold Circle / B to duck while driving.

    Reward / unlocks: Gun shops, loadouts

  6. 6

    Know your criminal profile (one tier is permanent)

    SetupBothHigh priority

    Jason and Lucia each have their own profile, set by how violently you play. Professional (start) and Hardened: incapacitating shots (yellow markers) and stealth takedowns refill Focus, you can surrender at low wanted levels (R1 / RB), and police mostly use tasers. Violent: headshots refill Focus instead. Psycho: no surrender, more lethal police. All four can be moved between. Keep going as a Psycho after the cracked-icon warnings and you become Irredeemable for the rest of the game: no Focus refills at all, police shoot at any crime, no surrender, and the video says some endings are no longer available for Psycho or Irredeemable. Lower your profile with good deeds: helping strangers, petting stray cats, throwing away trash. Newer videos agree: Irredeemable cannot be undone on that save and changes the story and endings, so make a manual save before going on a spree (JorRaptor). Full details on the Criminal profile page.

    Gun Nut house next door: sniper, LMG, rocket launcher, body armor

    LootJasonHigh priority

    The house about 0.1 mi from Jason's, reachable straight after Pipework. Kick the door in upstairs; the owner is armed. Inside: the unique Gun Nut SMS-01 sniper, a wallet, frag grenades, a Mustang revolver, a safe in the first bedroom (about $250), a scoped 556 rifle on the owner, a shotgun behind the shower and a rocket launcher in the bathroom (climb into the tub), body armor on the bed, a light machine gun, and adrenaline in the garage. You can only carry so many long guns, so pick between the rifle, LMG and rocket launcher (store the rest in your trunk). Two videos show it; one says some players find the house empty, and Detonated still lists the Gun Nut SMS-01 as not found.

    MoneyJasonHigh priority

Approach by boat, thin them out with a sniper, then loot the main hut: the Kingfishers W-22 (unique, built-in silencer) is in the weapon crate, there is cash under the floorboards, and two non-unique guns (Duke 556, Laird 350) lie around the camp.     Before Moses: raid La Cascara Motel for a free Dominator

    StealthStealth tips
    • Go at night: it is easier to stay unseen (Jay Dunna).
    • Crouch along the south side of the 2nd floor and use Focus to see who is looking; two gang members need taking down quietly, the second from the side path by the grow operation (Its Shatter).
    • Take guards from behind: choke them out, or pistol-whip with a suppressed pistol out (Jay Dunna).
    • If the alarm goes up, pull them through the choke point north of the safe room and drop them with knee and elbow shots (Its Shatter).
    • Leave by dropping through the broken floors; the police arrive after the noise (Jay Dunna).
    LootLuciaHigh priority

    After Girl's Trip, Moses' first registration is free, so bring him the best car you can. The derelict La Cascara Motel near Gellhorn Outfitters (Kolt & Associates gang) has the red/black Vapid Firebug Dominator out front: nitrous, no tracker. Stealth route: 2nd floor south side, metal stairs on the west to the 3rd floor, the caved-in ceiling as a ramp to the 4th. The Lieutenant's room safe is 6969 (about $3,000) and a gun case on the way to the north stairwell holds the Scrapper VF5C, a suppressed SMG that is great for non-lethal takedowns. Leave through the hole in the floor past the locked gate, take the Dominator and jump the water from the trailer park; Moses is about 0.3 miles away. If it is not your free car it costs about $3,000 to register. Three videos agree on the code and the car. Jay Dunna adds a grenade launcher on the top floor and an LMG on a lower floor, and says the Dominator respawns out front a few days later if you leave it. Go at night and escape by dropping through the broken floors.
