Rain, Lasswell, Fina, Nichol + Jake

Almost every build guide and video agrees your four main characters are the best story party. Rain tanks, Lasswell staggers, Fina heals and Nichol buffs, and none of their jobs overlap. This page covers which Visions to equip and level, what to buy and equip, how to open each fight, and when to bring in Jake.

Build path:
The safe pick: each character's Vision matches their role (WoL, Firion, Leah, Tronn). Strong from Chapter 3 on and easy to level.

How the four fit together

Each turn has a job

Nichol buffs the party with Formations (Assail gives +30% stagger power). Rain draws enemy attacks with Multi-Cover. Lasswell breaks the enemy. Fina heals, or passes her turn. Once the enemy is staggered, everyone goes all out: Limit Bursts like Infinity Drive deal +50% to staggered targets.

Two rules every source agrees on

1. Keep a defender and a healer in every fight. Build your damage around them.
2. Elements change from boss to boss. Jobs don't. Swap Visions to hit weaknesses, but never give up Rain's Cover, Lasswell's stagger or your healing to do it.

When Jake comes in

Jake has the highest physical damage in the cast, plus Wind attacks of both kinds and Lightning. He's built around Luck (see his tab).

His kit, and how to use it

  • Tactic: Fluid Warfare (Lv 19): after a Formation he gets one extra action per turn. Use it on a safe turn in long fights. Skip it against small trash fights.
  • Formations: Assail (+30% stagger power for the party, the default opener) · Charge (+30% crit chance) · Bulwark (defense) · Regroup (Lv 34, party attack).
  • Stratagem (11): the enemy takes +30% stagger for 3 turns. Dispel / Dispelga remove Reflect and Protect. Libra shows weaknesses. Faith and Slow.
  • Water up to Waterga, plus Aqua Blast. Key against Ignis, the Emperor and Dark Fina.
  • God of Water's Blessing (Lv 40) makes buffs last longer.

    Turn 1 against a boss (core party)

    1. Nichol: Formation: Assail (+30% stagger). In long fights, put up Fluid Warfare first on a safe turn. Once you have First Strike he acts before the enemy.
    2. Rain: Provoke or Multi-Cover, then Protectga if it isn't up yet. With Wilhelm, add Guard.
    3. Lasswell: Null Moon (if his LB is full) → Hone Senses → Placidity → big stagger hit. Hit the weakness Nichol revealed with Libra.
    4. Fina: Shellga or Light Veil before big magic. Otherwise Pass the Torch to Lasswell to speed up the break.

    Next turns

    Before the break

    • Nichol uses Stratagem (+30% stagger taken for 3 turns).
    • Rain attacks with Tackle or the weak element.
    • Watch the stagger preview to see who can finish the break. Don't just add up percentages in your head, because some effects overlap.
    • Against groups, try to stagger all of them on the same turn (a sweeping stagger gives the party bonus actions).

    During the stagger

    • Switch to damage. Don't spend more MP on stagger.
    • Fire your Limit Bursts now: Infinity Drive does +50% to staggered targets.
    • Pick a Resonance: Healing Wind if you need it, a damage Resonance if you're healthy.
    • Staggering a boss also cancels its big telegraphed attack (Dystopia, Crushing Breath and so on).

    Against trash fights, just attack. Every guide says not to run the full buff routine against weak enemies.

    Expert mode notes

    • On Expert your damage is 80% of Normal, and enemies have more HP, bigger stagger bars, more speed and harder hits. You also earn ×1.5 MRP.
    • You can change difficulty outside of battle: Options → Gameplay → Difficulty. Dropping to Casual for one boss and switching back is allowed.
    • Suggested levels on Expert (KultZ): Ch 3 about 20–28, Ch 4 high 20s to low 30s. Chapter 5 bosses are Lv 33–39.

    The forge opens with the quest Forger of Wills: talk to the Gossiping Girl near the Dilmagia entrance (Ch 5), then visit Aileen on the airship (Ch 6 per games.gg). Each weapon costs 1 Adamantite.

    Three separate kinds of progress

    Character level

    Unlocks the character's own moves (for example, Nichol gets Fluid Warfare at Lv 19).

    Mastery Rank (MRP)

    Belongs to that character + Vision pair. Rain mastering Firion doesn't help Fina. Each rank gives either an ability or a permanent stat boost.

    Awakening ★ (Magicite)

    Unlocks more of the Vision's skills: Shard → 1★, Magicite → 2★, Cryst → 3★. Getting a Vision to MR 9 does not unlock its stars.

    AP: your ability budget

    • Each character has 8 ability slots and an AP pool of about 100–108 by Chapter 3. Each Vision adds +8 AP at MR 4 and again at MR 9.
    • The equipped Vision's own abilities cost no AP. AP only pays for Mastery abilities you borrow from other Visions. Attacks cost MP, not AP.
    • Spirit of X (MR 9) bundles all of a Vision's Mastery abilities into one slot, but costs 90–130 AP. It's only worth it if you want most of that bundle.
    • If you're a few AP short, a quick MR 4 on a fresh Vision (+8) is cheaper than pushing another Vision to MR 9.

    KultZ's AP budgets: the starter setup is under 100 for everyone. The full setup is about 150 for Rain and Nichol, about 165 for Lasswell and about 170 for Fina. Optional extras push it to 205–235.

    Farming MRP

    • Chapter 5 right now: Cactuar in Dirnado (west of the Onion Knight sanctum) or Zoldaad (volcano area), 220 MRP each. Stagger it first, because it stops dodging while staggered.
    • Later: Gigantuar in Gronoa / Pharm (600 MRP).
    • Sparkling pots: give them the item they ask for instead of attacking. Each pot is a one-off bonus, from about 180 to 1,800 MRP (Elixir pot = 1,800).
    • Bonuses: ×1.3 for finishing during stagger, ×1.5 on Expert, ×2 Mastery Pendant. Victoria's MRP +50% doesn't stack with the Pendant.
    • Equip a Vision on every party member first, because benched members earn MRP too. Skip Metal enemies when farming MRP: they give lots of EXP but little MRP.
