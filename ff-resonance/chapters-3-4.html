Final Fantasy Resonance: Chapters 3 & 4

Story recap, walkthrough and combat cheat sheet, compiled from fan guides (Nov 2026)

Story so far

The big picture: Rain and Lasswell, two young knights of Grandshelt, are trying to protect the world's elemental Crystals from a group called the Sworn. Each Sworn member goes after a Crystal, and each chapter's shrine is its own fight. Chapter 1 was Earth (Belmodar), Chapter 2 was Wind (Aether), and Chapter 3 is Water (Alluvion).

Visions are crystallized essences of past FF heroes (Tidus, Squall, Terra, Noctis and others). You equip them to give characters jobs and abilities. Espers are summons. Fina gains the power to call them after the Siren fight.

Chapter 1, "Tailing the Truth"

Rain and Lasswell investigate the Earth Shrine, fight Belmodar, and get their first Visions (Leah and Tronn) at Rain's home in Mitra. In Grandshelt Castle they meet Hell's Rider and an Umbra you can't beat. Fina joins them. At the Kolobos Ruins they find Dr. Lazarov's lab (remember him for Chapter 4). The chapter ends with Siren at Grandport, and then they set sail.

Chapter 2, "Eternal Blue"

The party travels to Dirnado. Lid, an engineer competing for the title of "Cid", joins at the Dilmagia shipyard. They cross Mt. Thunderclap and the Dwarven Forge, rescue Borin from Mobliz Shipyard (with a 15-minute escape), and beat Aether at the Wind Shrine. The chapter ends aboard Lid's new airship.

Chapter 3 story: the full version

Where you are: Olderion, the "Aquapolis," on the Continent of Water. A family of Wardens of the Waters protects the city, and the city's Water Crystal is the next one the Sworn are after.

Chapter villain: Alluvion, the Sworn member of Water.

Story beats and walkthrough

1Shot out of the sky

Lid's new airship starts losing altitude ("I can't maintain altitude!") and crash-lands on a beach south of Olderion. The party walks north into the city.

  • Talk to the soldier in the central plaza and the youth in the north plaza (yellow "!"), then find Nichol on the northwest canal pier.
  • Nichol explains that the Water Maiden has been taken into the Cave of Waters.
  • Olderion loot: Rock Piercer, Jeweled Ring, Mirage Robe (4 shards, 6 chests). The Sanctum of Light has the Tidus, Firion and Squall Visions, but you can only get them on a later visit.
2The Cave of Waters: rescue Luka · rec. Lv 20

Nichol joins the party inside the cave. You fight your way through to reach his sister.

  • Golden Eagles (Lv 24) attack on the switchback road outside.
  • There are three raft areas. Hit the switch in each to flood the water so the raft can move.
  • Save points are on 1F and on 2F before the boss. Loot: Total Calisthenics, Ninja Gear.
  • Boss: Wave Lopros. Luka is freed afterward.
3Cleansing the city's water

Olderion's water flows through three control points, and Luka has to purify each one. Walk her to the bridge southeast of the central plaza, then to the tip of the northeast arm by the orphanage, then to the west corridor past the north plaza. These are story scenes, with no fights.

At the third point, El runs up with bad news: monsters are back in Old Town, where his wife Arsha is trying to get people out.

4Old Town under siege

The first fight happens with El alongside the defenders. Then Lasswell and Nichol stay behind to hold the line, and Rain, Fina and Lid go on alone.

  • Point of no return: you can't come back through the gate until the event is over.
  • Lid doesn't have a Vision yet, so equip her with one here.
  • Boss: Water Dragon. Afterward Luka says, "We must head to Lake Dorr and check on the God of Water." Lasswell and Nichol rejoin the party.
5Lake Dorr: the God of Water goes mad · rec. Lv 23

Go back through the Cave of Waters and take the west exit through the north terraces. At the lake, Leviathan, the God of Water that Luka was worried about, has gone mad and attacks. Once you calm it, Luka thanks the party.

Leviathan becomes an Esper later, through Nichol's character quest "Nichol's Duty," which opens after Chapter 4.

6The night of the flood

The party rests at the Olderion inn (100 Gil). That night the city floods and Alluvion appears. In the chaos, Archwitch Fina takes Fina's place in the party.

Nichol says the way into the Water Shrine starts at Waterfall Way, northwest of the city, and El joins to guide them.

7Waterfall Way, the Altar, and Rain's collapse · rec. Lv 24

Climb Waterfall Way to the Altar of the Water Maiden. There's no boss, but on 3F Gilgamesh shows up and asks about the Genji Gloves. At the altar, El explains that the road to Warden's Cape goes through the town of Amore.

When you reach Amore, Rain collapses. Archwitch Fina says plainly that what she did for him was only a temporary fix, and that he'll die if they don't find a real solution. This is a big character moment: the healing on Rain's arm is running out.

  • Waterfall Way loot: Devout's Armor, Guardian Sword, Aquatic Veil. Amore loot: Morning Star, Aegis Robe. Amore doesn't sell Tents.
8Warden's Cape: a bridge from the sea

On the coast, Nichol uses the Wardens' magic ("I shall use the magic"), and a stone bridge rises out of the sea to the Water Shrine. At the entrance, Alluvion ambushes the party, traps everyone in water bubbles, and leaves to go after the Crystal. There's no fight here. El runs into the shrine alone, and Rain says, "We have to hurry!"

9The Water Shrine · rec. Lv 26
  • Activate the seal pedestals in Areas 3, 4 and 2 to open the route. Take the boat to the first switch.
  • In the crate room, hit the switches left, right, left. That opens a hidden chest with the R'lyeh Text. Lower the water to reach the lone box. Build crate bridges to get the Calm Armlet, which you'll want for the boss.
  • The lift in Area 1 takes you down to B2, where the last save point before the boss is.
  • Boss: Alluvion.
10The price of the Divine Signet

In the fight, Alluvion uses the Divine Signet, which Charms and Berserks your party. After the battle there's a long scene: using the Divine Signet drained Archwitch Fina's power, and she has to leave for a while. Fina comes back in her place. The party returns to Olderion, goes after Arsha, and boards a ship at the port for the Zoldaad Empire.

What the guides don't explain yet

No guide says what Alluvion ultimately wants, what the Divine Signet really is, what happens to the Water Crystal, or why you have to chase Arsha. The guides only cover the gameplay steps for these parts, so you'll have to play the cutscenes to find out.

Chapter 3 bosses

Wave Lopros · Cave of Waters · Lv 20 · 21,000 HP · stagger 600
  • Weak to ICE. It fights alongside Azure and Land Snappers.
  • Devour eats one of your party, and "lures in more prey" calls in more enemies. Stagger it to stop both.
  • It gets stronger after each meal and heals itself with Cannibalize (2,000 HP).
  • Ice users: Lasswell (Blizzard at Lv 1), or the Noctis, Terra, Tronn and Victoria Visions. Drop: Water Scroll I.
Water Dragon · Old Town · Lv 21 · 24,000 HP · stagger 600
  • Weak to LIGHTNING. It only takes half damage from Water.
  • Its big attack is Bubble Breath (three Water hits). Charge can Paralyze, and Octo-Strike hits random targets 8 times.
  • Lightning users: Sakura (Lv 3), or the Noctis and Tronn Visions. Drop: Water Scroll I.
Leviathan · Lake Dorr · Lv 23 · 40,000 HP · stagger 1,000
  • Weak to LIGHTNING. Resists Water.
  • Kill the Orbs of Light first to weaken it. It enrages if the fight drags on.
  • Tsunami and Killer Tsunami each hit 5 times, and staggering it stops them. Constrict Paralyzes, so equip a Ribbon. Drop: Magicite Shard.
Alluvion · Water Shrine B2 · Lv 26 · 48,000 HP · stagger 850
  • Weak to LIGHTNING. Immune to Water.
  • Prism cores absorbed! Each core it absorbs makes its special attack stronger, so keep your HP high.
  • The Divine Signet inflicts Charm and Berserk. Equip Monk's Prayer Beads against Charm and a Calm Armlet against Berserk (a Ribbon covers both), and bring Remedies.
  • Maelstrom hits the whole party. Graceful Deluge removes your Protect and Shell.
  • Drops: Azure Amulet, Water Scroll II.

Chapter 3 combat rules

  • Bring Lightning for 3 of the 4 bosses. Use Ice for Wave Lopros.
  • Never cast Water. Every boss here resists it or is immune.
  • Save your strongest hits for the stagger bar, because staggering a boss cancels its most dangerous attacks.
  • Bring protection against Paralysis (Leviathan) and Charm/Berserk (Alluvion).

Chapter 4 story: the full version

Where you are: the Zoldaad Empire, an industrial nation run by an emperor and protected by an army. Its people are oppressed, and a rebel army operates in tunnels under the capital.

Antagonists: the Emperor of Zoldaad, Ignis (a fire-wielding Sworn-tier boss you meet again in Chapter 7), and Dr. Lazarov, the scientist whose lab you found in the Kolobos Ruins in Chapter 1.

Leader of the rebel army. He seems laid-back and easily distracted, but he'll do anything to free his people. His character quest is "Token Legacy."

The ruler the rebels are fighting. Ghostly praetorian guards protect him in the throne room.

Dr. Lazarov

From the Kolobos Ruins lab in Chapter 1. He fights with his own test subjects and can inject "Frenzied Blood."

Story beats and walkthrough

1Arrival and the Mozul train

The ship from Olderion lands you in Zoldaad territory. Head southeast to Mozul Station and talk to the railway officer to board. As in Chapter 2, the region is locked when you arrive.

On board, talk to every companion and to Gilgamesh, who is riding along. Then imperial soldiers storm the train from the left. Run right toward the back of the train. You only have to fight if they catch you (or farm them if you like).

  • Mozul Station: 1 Tablet Shard.
2The imperial capital

When you arrive at Capital Station, the opening scenes show Zoldaad under the empire's control. A notice board announces "reconstruction" and the reopening of the Colosseum. Look around the Castle Plaza, Capital Market, Imperial Residences and Slums. The Commercial District and the Slums are the parts that matter for the story.

  • At the inn, a man tells you about an Esper, and there's a chest behind the counter.
  • Then meet your contact in the alley behind the Imperial Hotel.
  • The Sanctum of Light here has the Shantotto, Cecil and Y'shtola Visions, but they need a later visit. Titan is optional: take a Hammer of Gaia from the Colosseum to free him in the desert.
3Underground: the rebel hideout

The contact leads you into the Underground Tunnel. Go east to the Rebel Hideout, talk to the rebel soldiers, and go to Jake's room in the northwest. There you "share your thoughts with Jake," which is where he explains the rebellion and the party agrees to help. You sleep at the hideout inn, and Lasswell has a quiet scene before the big operation.

  • Tunnel loot: Lucky Bell (2 shards). Hideout loot: Robe of Roses (2 shards, 3 chests).
4The weapons factory: a fight you can't win

The factory stands where the Fire Shrine used to be. When you arrive, Ignis challenges you. The first fight is scripted (he has 999,999 HP and uses Blistering Hellfire), and it ends on its own with the party beaten.

  • Puzzle: crystals raise and lower platforms, and you rotate catwalks over the lava. From the starting layout, press the left control twice, the middle once and the right once. That connects the walkway. Then activate the large pedestals and drop the ladder next to the big machine to make a shortcut back.
  • Loot: Hoodwink Garb, Magicite, Knife of Blind ×3, Mist Potion ×3, Lightning Scroll I ×3 (2 shards, 5 chests).
  • At the end of the factory, Ignis blocks the way again, and this time you can win. Boss: Ignis.
5Back at the hideout: Lasswell's moment

The rebels react to the victory. You get a dialogue choice with Lasswell, and each answer gives a permanent stat bonus:

  • "My magic seems more effective": +10 MP
  • "The battle was intense, it changes you": +3 Strength
  • "I feel like you move faster": +3 Speed

Then go to Jake's room, where he lays out the plan to sneak into Zoldaad Castle.

6The dungeon: free Shera

The break-in starts badly: you begin in a prison cell. Escape it, climb the central staircase, go north and then west, and defeat the soldier carrying the Dungeon Key. Use it to open every cell. A burly prisoner gives you an X-Potion and a Mist Ether, and you find Shera and free her. Leave through the northern passage.

  • Dungeon loot: Pulse Cannon (2 shards).
7Through the castle: the captain's choice

When you enter the castle proper, a scene plays in which Ignis appears to briefly join your side. That's a surprising twist, but the guides don't explain it. Head west and beat the Ghost Soldiers in the required fight. Then go upstairs to the captain's room at the far end.

  • Ask "Captain, aren't you tired of the situation in Zoldaad?" and he hands over the Throne Room Key without a fight.
  • The other answer starts a battle instead.
  • Loot: Kikokusenga, plus Hairpin of Compassion from behind a painting (2 shards). There's another shard by the horse statue at the stairs.
8The throne room

You confront the Emperor of Zoldaad himself, flanked by ghostly guards. Boss: Emperor of Zoldaad. After the fight, check the left side of the throne for the Kaiser Sword, a new weapon for Rain.

9The roof: Evan's duel

Take the west exit up to the top of the castle. Evan challenges Rain to a one-on-one duel and gives up once he's taken enough damage. He only has 4,000 HP, so it's more a story moment than a hard fight.

10Dr. Lazarov's last experiment

After more cutscenes, Dr. Lazarov shows up. This is the same scientist behind the Kolobos Ruins lab and The Hunger from Chapter 1. He throws his creations at you: The Hunger v5, Test Subject No. 9 and No. 999. Boss: Lazarov. Guides disagree on where the fight happens: either aboard the airship (the Enterprise, or the Invincible on Fextralife) or at the castle.

11Viva la revolución

With the Emperor and Lazarov beaten, the rebellion wins. Completing the chapter unlocks the trophy "Viva la revolución." Jake officially joins the party with a final dialogue choice:

  • "Glad to have you. We're counting on you.": +30 HP
  • "Your guns will surely help us.": +3 Strength (guides recommend this one)
  • "Let's fight this world, together.": +3 Stamina

The party gets an airship. Chapter 5, "Scars of Time," opens with you flying it outside the capital. Use its radar to find the hidden continent of Mysidia (south and slightly east of Pharm) and Sakura's home. Zoldaad comes back in Chapters 5 and 8.

What the guides don't explain yet

No guide gives the full story on: why Shera is imprisoned and how she's connected to the Emperor, why Ignis seems to switch sides, whether the Emperor is killed or was being controlled, who Evan really is, what Lazarov is after, or what happens to the Fire Crystal. Chapter 4 bosses

Ignis · Weapons Factory · Lv 27 · 56,000 HP · stagger 1,000 · 10,000 gil
  • Weak to WATER. Takes half damage from Fire.
  • He enrages when hit, and even more when you hit his weakness. Keep healing high while you pile on Water.
  • Firaga/Firestorm hits the whole party. Summon Carbuncle to Reflect it.
  • Other moves: Blistering Hellfire, Colossus Cleave, Charge, Body Slam.
  • Water users: Nichol (learns it at Lv 3), or the Shantotto, Squall and Terra Visions. Drop: Solar Pendant.

Your Chapter 3 habit of avoiding Water flips here: Water is now your main weapon.

Emperor of Zoldaad · Throne room · Lv 30 · 52,000 HP · stagger 700 · 30,000 gil
  • Weak to WATER.
  • He summons ghostly Praetorians, so kill those first.
  • He uses Bio (Poison), Blindga and Gravity. A Star Pendant blocks Poison.
  • Hell's Pillar is his big Fire attack (3 hits). Carbuncle's Reflect helps against his Fire spells.
  • Drop: X-Ether.
Evan · Castle roof · Lv 30 · 4,000 HP · stagger 1,500

No weakness. He uses Cut, Stab, Blizzard and Thunder. It's a short duel, so just keep Rain healthy.

Dr. Lazarov · Lv 30 · 45,000 HP · stagger 700 · 10,000 gil
  • Weak to EARTH. Stone users: the Amelia Vision (from the Errand's End request) and the Onion Knight Vision (Dirnado).
  • He puts your party to Sleep. Keep your healer awake with a Headband.
  • He also uses Debrave, Deprotect, Aerora, Blizzara and Revenge Blast, plus "Frenzied Blood" injections.
  • Each test subject has its own weakness:
    • The Hunger v5 is weak to Light and Fire.
    • Test Subject No. 9 is weak to Water.
    • No. 999 is weak to Wind.

Chapter 4 combat rules

  • Switch to Water. Ignis and the Emperor are both weak to it, and Nichol is your Water caster.
  • Carbuncle's Reflect handles most of the Fire magic in this chapter.
  • Set up an Earth user (Amelia or Onion Knight) before the Lazarov fight, and bring a Headband for Sleep and a Star Pendant for Poison.
  • Stock up in the hideout: after you break into the castle, you can only resupply from the dungeon cells and chests.
