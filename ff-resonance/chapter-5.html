The chapter in one breath

Now that the party has their own airship, the Enterprise, they hunt for Mysidia, the Magiapolis, a city of magic hidden at sea. There they meet Sakura, a sage who looks like a young girl but talks like someone centuries old. They help with a medicine run to the snowy village of Sunioo and explore the Nameless Ruins. Then things get personal for one of their own, and the chapter turns into a trip back through time.

In the ruins a golden-armored warrior named Tellus blocks their way, and right after that Fina disappears. Archwitch Fina takes her place and warns that the Fina they know may be gone for good. Using a tome from the Arcane Library, the party goes inside Fina's psyche. There they see a war on Paladia seven hundred years ago, in which Archwitch Fina fought for the nation of Hess. They meet the Veritas and Umbra at a camp, beat Dark Fina, beat Tellus for real, and bring both Finas back. Next stop: Gronoa.

Who matters this chapter

Sakura

Sage of Mysidia · staff · magic attacker

She looks like a child but speaks like an elder, and nobody knows how old she really is. She hits harder with magic than anyone else at level 1, using Lightning, Dark and non-elemental spells.

Fina & Archwitch Fina

Medic (bow) · Saboteur (whip)

Fina's other self first showed up in Chapter 3, when she healed Rain's arm. This chapter is about who these two really are.

Tellus

Golden-armored warrior · weak to Wind

He fights by a code and keeps calling out whether he wants magic or martial skill from you. You meet him twice this chapter.

Jake

Commando · guns

He joins officially at the start of the chapter. He has both physical and magic Wind attacks, which makes him great against Slagworm and Tellus.

L

Lid

Pilot of the Enterprise

She learns her Limit Break, Enterprise Charge, in the opening scene, and her radar is how you find Mysidia.

U

Umbra

You lost to him in Chapter 1

He has a camp inside the memory world. The guides don't explain his goals yet.

Beat by beat

  1. A new ally and a new ship

    Zoldaad Castle → outside the capital

    After the revolution, Jake promises to stay with the party. Lid learns Enterprise Charge, and the Enterprise is now yours to fly anywhere in the world.

  2. A city you can't see

    Ocean south-southeast of Pharm

    Mysidia is hidden by magic, so flying over it shows nothing. Lid's detector and radar pulses are the only way to find it.

  3. Sakura

    Mysidia, west across the bridge

    The ageless sage agrees to guide the party. She sends them to the potion factory to pick up special medicine that the snowbound village of Sunioo badly needs.

  4. The snowfield and Sunioo

    North of Mysidia

    A huge Slagworm attacks on the snowy road in. After the medicine is delivered, Sakura has a quiet talk with the party about her magic and how long she has lived.

  5. The Nameless Ruins

    Southwest

    The party clears two ancient guardians and reaches the deepest room, where someone is waiting for them.

  6. Tellus, and Fina is gone

    Nameless Ruins, inner door

    Tellus overwhelms the party in a fight you can't win. Right after, Fina vanishes and Archwitch Fina takes her place. She warns them that the Fina they know may disappear for good.

  7. The Arcane Library

    Mysidia

    Sakura's library holds the answer, but five haunted books have to be beaten first. In the Forbidden Text Wing, a guardian protects the tome that opens a way into a person's mind.

  8. Into Fina's psyche

    Memory world, first visit

    The party follows Fina's memories through a world that isn't their own. A great gate stays sealed. Some of those memories belong to Sakura too, so they'll have to come back later.

  9. The Tower of Mysidia

    West side of the continent

    At the top of the tower Sakura shares her own memories. That's the missing piece that opens the rest of Fina's mind.

  10. Paladia, 700 years ago

    Memory world, second visit

    Now the memory world is a war zone. The party passes the Hessian base and reaches a camp with the Veritas, the "Sworn Eight", and Umbra. They learn that Archwitch Fina fought for Hess, against the Veritas of the Dark, with Bahamut at her side. After Rain and Archwitch Fina talk at the gate, it finally opens.

  11. Dark Fina, then Tellus

    The battlefield

    Following the flowers leads to Dark Fina. Once she's beaten, Fina and Archwitch Fina are both back in the party. Then Tellus returns, and this time you can win.

  12. Back to Sakura's house

    Mysidia

    The closing scenes play, and the next destination is Gronoa, where Chapter 6 begins.

What the guides don't explain yet: Umbra's and the Veritas' goals, the "Sworn Six" vs. "Sworn Eight" naming, which Crystal (if any) this chapter is about, and the full dialogue of Fina's memories. None of the sources describe these beyond the story summary.

I · TAKING TO THE SKY
Choicehigh priority

Talk to Jake at Zoldaad Castle

The chapter opens with Jake joining for good. Each answer gives him a different permanent stat:

  • "Glad to have you. We're counting on you." → +30 HP
  • "Your guns will surely help us." → +3 Strength (recommended)
  • "Let's fight this world, together." → +3 Stamina
Unlocks: Lid's Limit Break Enterprise Charge
Story

Board the Enterprise outside the capital

Leave the castle and the city. The airship is parked just outside. Go into its interior for a scene, and after that you can fly freely.

Trophy: The Enterprise
Optional

Airship detours (now or later)

  • Ifrit (Esper): a marked volcano near Zoldaad. Weak to Water.
  • Quarry Cave, on the western islet of Zoldaad: an Ancient Accessory chest. The south tip has Phoenix Pinion ×2.
  • Clive Vision: in the Sanctum of Light north of Grandshelt. There's a Power Stone just outside.
  • Olderion: Fuma Shuriken ×3 on Lake Dorr's north beach, Ring of Retaliation to the south, and the Rompebarreras weapon on the northwest island.
  • Sian (Pharm): a Tablet Shard by the tree near the shops, plus Phoenix Pinions, Ultra Potions and Ether+ hidden behind houses.

The Chamber of Arms (island northwest of Dirnado) has bosses around Lv 50 with two phases each. Wait until you're stronger.

Storyhigh priority

Find Mysidia

Fly over the ocean south and slightly east of the Kingdom of Pharm and use the radar pulse. Lid gives you hints, so keep scanning as you move. The city is hidden and you won't spot it just by flying over.

You don't need the High-Power Generator or any radar upgrades for this. Lid's story detector is enough.

III · THE NAMELESS RUINS
LootPuzzle

The statue chest (Tyr's Armor)

Examine the locked chest at the entrance first. Then activate the three statues in this order: Broken Sword (by the far save point) → Withered Horse (second area) → Darkness Sealed (next to the chest). Open the chest for Tyr's Armor. If you get the order wrong, start again from Broken Sword.

Also here: Magi-Blaster on the west stairs, and a Tablet Shard with Magicite in the upper-west room (2 shards in the ruins).

Bossguardian 1 of 2

Iron Giant (east room)

Use Lightning magic. It counters physical hits, and a Lightning spellblade still counts as physical. Keep your caster and healer up.

Bossguardian 2 of 2

Liquid Flame (west room)

Use Water or Ice, and never Fire, which heals it. It counters physical hits and puts Reflect on itself, so check its buffs before casting and remove Reflect with Dispel or Magicide. The room also has Vitalizing Fluid ×3.

StoryScripted

Tellus at the north door: a fight you can't win

Save first. This is a three-turn story battle that ends on its own, so don't waste rare items. The scenes that follow change your party.

Fina leaves the party and Archwitch Fina rejoins.

Missable

Go back to the boss room for the Impact Wand

After the scene, return to the room where you fought Tellus. A chest there has the Impact Wand, and it's easy to walk right past.

After the ruins, resting at the Olderion Inn starts Jake's quest Token Legacy.
IV · THE ARCANE LIBRARY
Setup

Before you enter: check your levels

One guide warns that an enemy here casts Level 5 Death, which kills anyone whose level is a multiple of 5. Make sure no one is at level 35 or 40 when you go in. (Only one source mentions this.)

Story0 / 5

Defeat the five haunted books

Open each marked book and win its fight. Just reading the description doesn't count. The books are: The Art of Death, The Song of Stone, Grimoire Esoterica, The Demesne of the Dead and For Whom the Giant Weeps. The counter has to reach zero.

Elevator loot: II → Mist Potion ×3 · III (down) → Mist Ether ×3 · V (down) → Knife of Blind ×2 · VI (up) → Genmato. There's a Tablet Shard between the tables in the first room.

Azure Pot (99,999 HP, 9,999 stagger, no weakness) drops an Elixir. It's not worth fighting it the normal way.

BossLv 36

Forbidden Text Wing: Byblos

Take the restored blue teleporter. The tome on the wall has its own guardian. Use Fire or Dark. Carbuncle's Reflect helps against its spells. Before the final bookshelf, check the balcony for a Tablet Shard (left) and the Book of Strength (right). Afterward, tell Sakura "yes" to go in.

V · FINA'S MEMORIES
StoryLoot here

First visit to Fina's psyche (no fights)

Follow Fina west through the scenes, check the large gate, then go to the cliff in the east. The gate will not open on this visit, and that's normal. Leave through the open book when the game tells you to.

Grab: a Tablet Shard by the chain, a Tablet Shard in the left shop, a Tablet Shard at the end of the forest path, and Ultra Potion ×2 in the center shop.

StoryAnswer order

Climb the Tower of Mysidia (west side)

Climb the stairways (there are save points along the way). At the top, when Sakura asks if you're ready, answer "not yet". Explore the top floor for the Guarding Gown (behind a column), then talk to her again and say "yes".

Also in the tower: Bullseye Mace behind the column on the left balcony, Magicite on the right balcony, Smelling Salts ×3 on the first floor, and 4 Tablet Shards.

Setuphigh priority

Move healing to someone else before the second visit

Fina isn't available, and this time there are real fights. Give a healing Vision such as Leah to a character who's in your active party, and check that the Cure command actually shows up. Bring Water and Light for the boss ahead (Nichol has Water, Sakura has Light).

StoryMalboro

Second visit: forest, camps, gate

  1. Cross the new bridge from the east into the forest, then head north.
  2. Beat the Malboro at the marked crossroads. It causes lots of status effects, so bring Remedies.
  3. Go through the Hessian base to Umbra's camp. Talk to every Veritas on the map, then to Umbra in his tent.
  4. Go back to the gate. Once the physical locks are gone, finish the Rain and Archwitch Fina conversation. You don't need a key.

Grab: Barrel Blazer (hidden chest on the left side of the camp), a Tablet Shard on the right side of the camp, and the Lunar Whip south of the Malboro crossroads.

Disputed missable

Get the psyche loot now

Guides disagree on whether you can come back later. To be safe, get all 4 Tablet Shards, the Barrel Blazer and the Lunar Whip during the story visit.

BossLv 38

The battlefield: follow the flowers to Dark Fina

Random fights start here. Check the flowers along the path. Then use Water and Light against her, and don't use Dark. Stagger her to cancel Dystopia. Paradise Lost boosts her Dark attacks, but it does not make her weak to Dark. Full card →

Drop: Ribbon · Trophy for having the full party back: Defying Fate
BossLv 39

Tellus, the real fight

Bring Wind in both physical and magic forms (Jake has both). Follow his call: "Show me majestic magic" means use magic, and "Show me magnificent martial skill" means use physical attacks. While he's fully broken (Full Break) he can't counter, so throw everything at him then. Stagger him to stop Crushing Breath. Some guides say you can field up to 8 party members and rearrange your formation here.

Finale

Back to Sakura's house

The closing scenes play, and your next objective is Go to Gronoa. No source names a point of no return, but check the Before you leave tab first.

Do these before you follow "Go to Gronoa"

No guide names a point of no return in Chapter 5. These are cautious steps for the places where sources disagree.

  • Collect everything in Fina's psyche during the story visit: 4 Tablet Shards, the Barrel Blazer and the Lunar Whip. Guides disagree on whether you can return.
  • Get the Impact Wand from the Tellus room in the Nameless Ruins.
  • Take the Phoenix Pinions from the Sunioo elder's house.
  • Finish Last Respite early. One guide says its hand-in on the airship deck closes late in the game, which would cost you the all-substories trophy.
  • Pick Magic +3 for Sakura and Strength +3 for Jake. These are one-time choices.
  • Make a separate save at the end of the chapter.

Easy to forget

The memory gate isn't a key puzzle

If it's sealed, you haven't finished the Tower of Mysidia yet. On the second visit, the Rain and Archwitch Fina conversation is what opens it.

Reading a book isn't beating it

Each of the five library books has to be opened and beaten. Watch the counter.

Your healer leaves

Fina is gone for the memory fights. Equip Leah (or another healer) on someone in your active party.

Tellus' calls

Same element, different attack type. A Wind spell and a Wind sword skill are not the same thing to him.

Character spotlight (no spoilers)

No guide or review talks about these characters' writing, so this is my read of how the chapter uses them, especially their fights and their role in the story.

Tellus

The golden-armored duelist

His role: he's the wall that stands between the party and the truth about Fina. You lose to him at the start of the mystery and beat him at the end of it, so he's how you can tell the party has grown.

Why it works

  • His personality is in his fight. He wants to see "magnificent martial skill" or "majestic magic," and he punishes you for giving him the wrong one. That says he's an honorable duelist without a single line of backstory.
  • Losing first makes winning matter. The three-turn loss in the ruins and the real win at the end give the chapter a clear beginning and end.
  • He's a different kind of fight from Ignis's rage-based one, so the bosses don't blur together.

Why it might not

  • Another fight you're meant to lose, right after Ignis did the same thing in Chapter 4. Seeing that twice in a row gets tiring.
  • "Big armored warrior with an honor code" is a very old archetype.
  • He blocks the way into the biggest story moment, so some players will just see him as an obstacle.

Goofy or not: his lines are big and theatrical. They'll land as noble if the voice actor plays them straight and as hammy if he chews the scenery. The mechanic gives him more dignity than the archetype does.

Sakura

The ageless sage

Her role: she's your guide to Mysidia's magic, and the person whose memories unlock the chapter's last act.

Why it works

  • She's useful right away. She has the best magic in the game at level 1 and brings Light, which you need soon.
  • Her choice is about respect. The best stat answer ("You are a wise person") rewards taking her seriously.
  • The story needs her for more than exposition: her memories at the tower are a real key.

Why it might not

  • "Looks like a child, talks like a grandmother" is an overused anime trope, and the joke can wear thin.
  • She shows up in a very busy chapter, alongside a new city, an airship and a mind trip, so she has less room to grow.

Goofy or not: the setup has goofy potential. Whether it lands depends on whether the script uses her age as a running joke or as weight. Her choice and her tower scene suggest weight.
