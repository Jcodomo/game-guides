Chapter 1

Tailing the Truth

Grandshelt: the Earth Shrine, your first Visions in Mitra, the castle rescue, Kol's desert, Kolobos Ruins and Siren's Tower.

Party
Rain, Lasswell; Fina joins after the castle
Objectives
19 (Fextralife)
Boss levels
4 to 40 (the Lv 35-40 fights come first; see the prologue step)
Bosses
Belmodar, Hell's Rider, Umbra (scripted), The Hunger, Siren
Side quests opened
4
Ends
Sailing from Grandport

Recommended build

RainTank

Vision: Warrior of Light (from the Kol sanctum)

He has the most HP; WoL adds defence, Provoke-style cover and Thunder/Stone Blade.

LasswellStagger breaker / Ice

Vision: Cloud (from the Cavern of Swords sanctum)

His own Ice handles Hell's Rider and the first Hunger; Cloud adds crit and Lightning for Siren.

FinaHealer / Light

Vision: Leah

Esper: Siren after the tower

Leah adds Nullify Charm (Siren) and more healing; her Light hits the revived Hunger.

  • Belmodar comes before any Visions: just use Rain's Fire.
  • Until WoL and Cloud are collected, put Leah on Fina and Tronn on Rain or Lasswell.
  • Tronn is the easy Lightning source for Siren; swap him onto whoever lacks Thunder.
  • Bring Monk's Prayer Beads or Leah's Nullify Charm against Siren's Charm.
  • Only three party members exist in this chapter, so the template's fourth (damage) slot is empty.

Source: ffresonanceguide Best Builds (Chapter 1 row: Rain + WoL, Lasswell + Cloud, Fina + Leah) and its boss pages; Game8 boss tips.

In order

  1. 1

    Prologue fights

    StoryLow priority

    Fextralife lists Cutting Edge Magitek Weapon (Lv 35) and Iron Giant (Lv 40, on a battlefield) before Belmodar, and Game8 lists a 'Complete the Prologue' objective, so these look like prologue battles. No saved source explains them in detail: unclear.

  2. 2

    Rain's first dialogue choice (before the Earth Shrine)

    ChoiceHigh priority

    Three answers, each a permanent stat: lead the way = +3 Strength, plan a retreat = +3 Stamina, stress teamwork = +3 Spirit. The build guide's pick for Rain is Stamina +3.

    Reward / unlocks: +3 Strength, Stamina or Spirit

  3. 3

    Earth Shrine: Belmodar

    StoryHigh priority

    Weak to Fire (and Dark). You have no Visions yet, so lean on Rain's Fire to fill the stagger bar and let Lasswell attack or heal. A soldier near the entrance restores the party. Flame Scroll I gives Lasswell a Fire hit too (Game8).

  4. 4

    Mitra: investigate Rain's house

    SetupHigh priority

    Open the locked interior door of the blue-roofed house and finish the scene to receive the Leah and Tronn Visions. Equip them straight away (the objective asks you to).

    Reward / unlocks: Leah (Medic) and Tronn (Breaker) Visions

  5. 5

    Grandshelt Castle: Hell's Rider

    StoryHigh priority

    The Rider is weak to Ice and Light; its Dark Skeletons are weak to Fire and Light. Leave one skeleton alive while you break the Rider: alone it gets a stronger special. Bring Antidotes for its Poison. Game8 lists it at Lv 6, 4,000 HP; Fextralife files it as a side-quest boss, so its status is a little unclear.

  6. 6

    Black Garb chest in the burning castle

    LootMedium priority

    After Hell's Rider, walk through the hidden wall beside the knight statue for the Black Garb. The castle closes for a while after this, but both guides say you can come back (Game8: from Chapter 4).

    Reward / unlocks: Black Garb

  7. 7

    Throne room: Umbra (scripted loss)

    StoryMedium priority

    Lv 17, 99,999 HP. This fight is meant to be lost; do not burn items on it. The real Umbra fight is in Chapter 7.

  8. 8

    Village of Kol: rest, then rent a chocobo

    StoryHigh priority

    Fina is now with you. Stay at the inn, then rent the chocobo (this unlocks the Kweh! trophy). Riding across the desert keeps you out of the optional Antlion at the whirlpool (Lv 20, weak to Water), which is much stronger than you at this point.

    Reward / unlocks: Chocobo; trophy Kweh!

  9. 9

    Grab the early Visions: Warrior of Light, Cloud, Terra

    SetupHigh priority

    Sanctums of Light: Warrior of Light is northwest of Kol, Cloud is southwest of the Cavern of Swords, Terra is southwest of Felicitas. Answers to the sanctum questions never matter. WoL goes on Rain and Cloud on Lasswell in the build guide.

    Reward / unlocks: Warrior of Light, Cloud and Terra Visions

  10. 10

    Felicitas: Secrets Unearthed

    OptionalMedium priority

    Side quest from Chloe in the item shop. It pays a Magicite Shard and Monk's Prayer Beads, which block Charm: exactly what you want for Siren.

    Reward / unlocks: Magicite Shard, Monk's Prayer Beads

  11. 11

    Kolobos Ruins: The Hunger, twice

    StoryHigh priority

    First form: Ice (Lasswell). It revives with Enhanced Wraith Soldiers and turns weak to Light (Fina, Leah's Banish). Do not spend everything on the first form. Game8 adds that staggering the first form gives free turns at the start of the second. Game8 also lists an Imposter Soldier here (weak to Ice and Dark; clear its Paralysis).

  12. 12

    Look for Emma's mom, then go to Grandport

    StoryMedium priority

    Finish the prisoner-rescue scenes in the ruins, then head to Grandport Harbor. The captain will not sail until Siren is dealt with.

  13. 13

    Siren's Tower: Genji Gloves chest

    StoryLow priority

    The north-end chest triggers a scripted Gilgamesh appearance and he takes the gloves. He always flees here, so save your MP. You get the gloves back later in his airship fight.

  14. 14

    Siren's Tower: Citrine Pot (third floor)

    OptionalMedium priority

    Use an Ether on the glowing pot (Item command, do not attack it) for 120 MRP. Each pot is a one-time reward, so equip the Visions you want to train first.

    Reward / unlocks: 120 MRP

  15. 15

    Boss: Siren

    StoryHigh priority

    Weak to Lightning (Tronn's Thunder; Game8 also suggests Cloud and WoL Thunder skills). Equip Charm protection before the portal: Monk's Prayer Beads (sold in Grandport, one in a tower chest) or Leah's Nullify Charm. Staggering her can interrupt Allure.

    Reward / unlocks: Siren Esper

  16. 16

    Fina's dialogue choice (after the tower)

    ChoiceHigh priority

    Rely on her = +10 MP, defensive role = +3 Stamina, praise her = +3 Spirit. The build guide's pick for Fina is MP +10.

    Reward / unlocks: +10 MP, +3 Stamina or +3 Spirit

  17. 17

    Talk to the Moogle of Light in Grandport twice

    SetupHigh priority

    This unlocks Vision awakening with Magicite, and the first talk gives a free Magicite Shard (your party-builds page).

    Reward / unlocks: Magicite Shard; awakening unlocked

  18. 18

    Quests at the port: Rear Support, Ahoy! and The Weight of Mercy

    OptionalLow priority

    Rear Support, Ahoy! (Zile, western pier, after Siren) and The Weight of Mercy (soldier in Grandport, after Kolobos Ruins). Dream of an Idealist starts from a soldier in Grandshelt Town Square.

    Reward / unlocks: Magicite Shard; Iwakirimaru; Flamberge

  19. 19

    Ride the ship out of Grandport

    StoryHigh priority

    Ends the chapter (trophy A Journey Begins). Give every current character a Vision before you board.

    Reward / unlocks: Trophy: A Journey Begins

In order

  1. 1

    Land in Dirnado and go to Dilmagia

    StoryMedium priority

    The region is mostly locked at first. Game8 suggests a little levelling so you keep pace with local enemies. Talk to the marked residents.

  2. 2

    Find Lid at the shipyard

    StoryHigh priority

    Lid joins: an engineer who fights with her Mechabo and brings Earth magic (Stone). Elven Maven (Silvia, between the shops) is available now.

    Reward / unlocks: Lid joins

  3. 3

    Dirnado Visions: Noctis, Onion Knight, Zidane

    SetupMedium priority

    Three Sanctums of Light are on this continent. Game8's boss tips already use Noctis and Zidane here, so at least some are reachable now (exact access timing is not stated). Onion Knight has Stone (Earth).

    Reward / unlocks: Up to three Visions

  4. 4

    Mt. Thunderclap: normal path or Lid's ladder

    OptionalLow priority

    Paying Lid 1,000 gil opens a shorter climb; it is a convenience only. If you use it, lower the ladder at the top so you can go back and forth.

  5. 5

    Boss: Liquid Thunder (mountain exit)

    StoryHigh priority

    Weak to Earth; Lightning does nothing despite the name. Use Lid's Stone (Game8 also names WoL's Stone Blade). Megavolt can Paralyse. Game8: give Fina the Cape of the Raiju to halve Lightning damage, and defend after it charges up.

  6. 6

    Optional: Ramuh (upper entrance)

    OptionalMedium priority

    Re-enter Mt. Thunderclap by the upper world-map entrance near Zidane's sanctum, follow the path to the gravestone and examine it. Lv 20, 24,000 HP (Game8), weak to Earth, Lightning nullified. Earth Scrolls from the Dwarven Forge help. Fine to leave for later.

    Reward / unlocks: Ramuh Esper

  7. 7

    Dwarven Forge: Mom Bomb

    StoryHigh priority

    Weak to Water (Fire is resisted). Water sources: Terra, Cloud's Water Surge, Zidane (Game8), or summon Siren. At low HP it turns invulnerable, explodes and leaves four Great Bombs: kill those to finish. Body Slam can Paralyse.

  8. 8

    Save before the upper shipyard event

    SetupHigh priority

    Make a manual save before the Mobliz story sequence: a 15-minute escape timer starts there.

  9. 9

    Mobliz Shipyard: rescue Borin and escape

    Time-sensitiveHigh priority

    Avoid the Guardian patrols (Lv 25, no special reward). In the control area the painted numbers 5, 8 and 3 add up to 16: pull lever 16 to power the elevator. Epimetheus is a weak mini-boss (Lightning); save your resources.

  10. 10

    Mobliz Shipyard: Citrine Pot (third floor)

    OptionalLow priority

    Another one-time Ether pot for 120 MRP. Probably best done before the timer starts; the sources do not say whether it is inside the timed section.

    Reward / unlocks: 120 MRP

  11. 11

    Boss: Adrastea (cargo train)

    StoryHigh priority

    Weak to Lightning and Water. It charges Wave Cannon: break it before the charge lands or brace. Game8 suggests Cloud (Ascension Resonance) and Physical Counter.

  12. 12

    Repair Mechabo (Arise, Mechabo!)

    OptionalMedium priority

    Accepted automatically back at the Dwarven Forge. Lyrical Riddles (Roy) opens at the same time, and Mechabo Evolved (Verun) follows.

    Reward / unlocks: Repaired Mechabo, Magicite Shard

  13. 13

    Wind Shrine: Viridian Pot

    OptionalLow priority

    In the pot room beside the top floor. Give it a Mist Ether for 200 MRP.

    Reward / unlocks: 200 MRP

  14. 14

    Boss: Aether (Wind Shrine)

    StoryHigh priority

    Weak to Ice; Wind is useless. Its summoned enemies are also weak to Ice. Game8: put Tronn, Terra or Noctis on everyone except Lasswell so the whole party has Ice, and use Tronn's Hibernal Fury Resonance.

  15. 15

    Return to Lid and board the airship

    StoryHigh priority

    The chapter ends when you follow the departure to Olderion, not when Aether falls. Shop first. Trophy: Those Who Unite the Skies.

    Reward / unlocks: Trophy: Those Who Unite the Skies

  • Three of four bosses are weak to Lightning; Wave Lopros is weak to Ice. Do not use Water.
  • Lightning sources named by your pages: Sakura (later), Noctis, Tronn; Cloud and WoL have Thunder skills.
  • The build guide's Chapter 3-5 row names Firion, Tidus and Squall, but your Chapter 3 page says the Olderion sanctum holding them only opens on a later visit. Until then use Cloud and Tronn.
  • After the flood the party changes: sources do not say who heals then, so move Leah to someone still in the party if needed (derived).
  • Status gear: Ribbon for Leviathan's Paralysis; Monk's Prayer Beads + Calm Armlet (or Ribbon) for Alluvion.

Source: Your Chapters 3-4 and Chapter 3 pages (boss rules), your party-builds page (core four), ffresonanceguide Best Builds (Chapters 3-5 row).

In order

  1. 1

    Lid's dialogue choice (start of chapter)

    ChoiceHigh priority

    The talk about her brother: promise support = +3 Strength, think about why he changed = +3 Magic, sibling bond = +3 Stamina. The build guide picks Strength +3 for Lid. Some guides label this scene differently; match the topic.

    Reward / unlocks: +3 Strength, Magic or Stamina

  2. 2

    Crash landing, then Olderion

    StoryMedium priority

    The airship comes down on the beach south of the city. Talk to the marked soldier and youth, then Nichol on the northwest canal pier. Loot: Rock Piercer, Jeweled Ring, Mirage Robe. The Sanctum of Light here (Tidus, Firion, Squall) only opens on a later visit (your Chapter 3 page).

  3. 3

    Cave of Waters: raft switches

    StoryHigh priority

    Nichol joins. There are three raft areas: hit each glowing switch to change the water level so the boat can move. Save points on 1F and before the boss. Loot: Total Calisthenics, Ninja Gear. Golden Eagles (Lv 24) roam the road outside.

    Reward / unlocks: Nichol joins

  4. 4

    Boss: Wave Lopros

    StoryHigh priority

    Lv 20, weak to Ice. It brings Azure Snappers (weak to Lightning) and Land Snappers (Wind) and eats them, or a party member, to heal and grow stronger. Kill the prey before Devour and stagger it to stop the eating.

  5. 5

    Purify the three waterways with Luka

    StoryMedium priority

    Bridge southeast of the central plaza, tip of the northeast arm by the orphanage, west corridor past the north plaza. Story scenes only. Medicinal Mission (Sarah, then Ilias) opens after the cave.

  6. 6

    Old Town: shop and loot before the gate

    Point of no returnHigh priority

    Once through the gate you cannot leave until the event ends. Chests and enemy scans here: Game8 says the area cannot be revisited, ffresonanceguide calls that disputed. Open all three visible chests and scan enemies now.

  7. 7

    Old Town: give Lid a Vision

    SetupHigh priority

    Lasswell and Nichol stay behind, so only Rain, Fina and Lid go on. Lid has no Vision yet: equip one with Lightning (Tronn, Noctis) so you can hit the Water Dragon.

  8. 8

    Boss: Water Dragon

    StoryHigh priority

    Lv 21, weak to Lightning; Water does half damage. Bubble Breath is three Water hits (keep Shell ready), Charge can Paralyse, Octo-Strike hits random targets eight times.

  9. 9

    Boss: Leviathan at Lake Dorr

    StoryHigh priority

    Lv 23, weak to Lightning, resists Water. Destroy the Orbs of Light to open it up, and again when they return. Stagger stops Tsunami / Killer Tsunami; Constrict Paralyses (Ribbon). This fight does not give you the Esper: that comes from Nichol's Duty later.

  10. 10

    Rest at the Olderion inn

    SetupHigh priority

    100 gil. The city floods that night and the party changes: Fina is replaced for the rest of the chapter (story spoiler, kept vague here). Re-check who your healer is.

  11. 11

    Waterfall Way to the Altar

    StoryMedium priority

    Recommended Lv 24. No boss. Gilgamesh shows up on 3F asking about the Genji Gloves. Loot: Devout's Armor, Guardian Sword, Aquatic Veil.

  12. 12

    Town of Amore

    StoryLow priority

    A story scene with Rain. The shop sells no Tents. Loot: Morning Star, Aegis Robe. The Tide from the Sky (soldier) opens here.

  13. 13

    Warden's Cape: the bridge from the sea

    StoryMedium priority

    Nichol raises a stone bridge to the Water Shrine. An ambush follows, but no fight.

  14. 14

    Water Shrine: crates and water levels

    StoryHigh priority

    Recommended Lv 26. Lower the water with the nearby glowing control, push the crates into a line, then raise the water and cross. Activate the seal pedestals in Areas 3, 4 and 2.

  15. 15

    Water Shrine: R'lyeh Text and Calm Armlet

    LootHigh priority

    In the crate room hit the switches left, right, left for a hidden chest with the R'lyeh Text. Build the crate bridges to reach the Calm Armlet, which blocks Berserk for the boss. Also: Scale Mail, Piercing Mace.

    Reward / unlocks: R'lyeh Text, Calm Armlet

  16. 16

    Water Shrine: Viridian Pot

    OptionalLow priority

    Area 2, first-floor railed platform. Mist Ether for 200 MRP.

    Reward / unlocks: 200 MRP

  17. 17

    Boss: Alluvion (B2)

    StoryHigh priority

    Lv 26, 48,000 HP, weak to Lightning and immune to Water. Destroy Prism Cores before it absorbs them (each makes its special stronger). The Divine Signet Charms and Berserks: Monk's Prayer Beads plus the Calm Armlet, or a Ribbon, and Remedies. Graceful Deluge strips Protect and Shell.

    Reward / unlocks: Azure Amulet, Water Scroll II

  18. 18

    Nichol's dialogue choice (after the shrine)

    ChoiceHigh priority

    Keep giving strategy advice = +30 HP (four sources; Game8 says +10), see us as a team = +3 Magic, speak freely = +3 Speed. The build guide picks Speed +3.

    Reward / unlocks: +30 HP, +3 Magic or +3 Speed

  19. 19

    Optional: Carbuncle in the Ruby Cave

    OptionalMedium priority

    Read the epitaph, then find the three sparkling fragments (ponds, east coast, the rise by the waterfall) and return to the epitaph. No battle. Carbuncle's Reflect is very useful against the Fire magic in Chapter 4.

    Reward / unlocks: Carbuncle Esper

  20. 20

    Go after Arsha, then sail to Zoldaad

    StoryHigh priority

    Finish the city scenes and take the southern port. Trophy: Olderion Blues.

    Reward / unlocks: Trophy: Olderion Blues

Rules every source agrees on

Protection (Rain), Stagger (Lasswell), recovery (Fina) and buffs or damage (Nichol, Sakura). Rain, Lasswell, Fina and Nichol are the cross-source story party.

Swap Visions to hit each boss's weakness, but never give up Rain's cover, Lasswell's stagger or your healing to do it.

One guide checked that matching a Vision's role to the character gives no extra bonus; most guides still pick by role because it keeps the job working.

Joined party members get MRP even when not in the active four, so keep a Vision equipped on everyone. Guests do not.

Character level unlocks the character's own skills; Mastery Rank belongs to a character + Vision pair; awakening stars come from Magicite (Shard 1 star, Magicite 2, Cryst 3) and are separate from MR.

The equipped Vision's own abilities are free; AP only pays for abilities borrowed from other Visions. Each Vision adds +8 AP at MR 4 and again at MR 9.

Build the break with buffs and weakness hits, then switch to damage and Limit Bursts (Infinity Drive does +50% to staggered targets). Staggering also cancels the big telegraphed attacks.
