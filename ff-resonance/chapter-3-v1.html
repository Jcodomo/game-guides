Final Fantasy Resonance: Chapter 3, “Wardens of the Aquapolis”

Olderion, the Continent of Water · 4 story bosses · 15 Tablet Shards · ends heading for Zoldaad

The main storyline in one paragraph

Lid's airship goes down on the way to Olderion, the water city whose Water Crystal is under threat. The Warden of the Waters, Nichol, needs help: the Water Maiden Luka is trapped in the Cave of Waters. Rain's group frees her by beating Wave Lopros, then walks Luka through the city to purify its waterways. Monsters overrun Old Town (Water Dragon), and the trail leads to Lake Dorr, where a maddened Leviathan attacks. That night the city floods and Alluvion, the chapter's real villain, shows up. Archwitch Fina takes Fina's place in the party. The group goes up Waterfall Way to the Altar, crosses at Warden's Cape on a stone bridge Nichol raises from the sea, and beats Alluvion deep in the Water Shrine. The chapter ends with the party going after Arsha and sailing for the Zoldaad Empire.

Walkthrough, beat by beat

1Crash landing, then Olderion
  • The airship comes down on the beach south of Olderion. Walk to the city.
  • Talk to the townsfolk, then to Nichol on the pier by the north-west canal. Then talk to the Warden of the Waters.
  • Sanctum of Light: the Tidus, Firion and Squall Visions are here, but they need a later visit.
  • Olderion loot: Rock Piercer, Jeweled Ring, Mirage Robe. 4 shards, 6 chests.
2Cave of Waters: save the Water Maiden · rec. Lv 20
  • There are 3 raft areas. Hit each switch to flood the water so the raft can move.
  • Save points are on 1F and just before the boss on 2F.
  • Golden Eagles (Lv 24) attack on the road there.
  • Loot: Total Calisthenics, Ninja Gear. 3 shards, 5 chests.
  • Boss: Wave Lopros. Luka is freed afterward.
3Purify the waters with Luka
  • There are 3 flow points: the bridge SE of the central plaza, the tip of the NE arm by the orphanage, and the west corridor past the north plaza.
  • At the third point, El warns you that monsters are back in Old Town.
4Old Town: defeat the monsters
  • Point of no return: once you go through the gate, you can't leave until the event is over. Shop first.
  • Lid has no Vision yet, so give her one here.
  • Boss: Water Dragon.
5Lake Dorr: Leviathan
  • Go back through the Cave of Waters to reach the lake.
  • Boss: Leviathan + Orbs of Light. Luka thanks the party afterward.
  • Leviathan can be recruited as an Esper later (50 MP, finisher: Tsunami).
6The flood, Waterfall Way, Amore, Warden's Cape
  • Stay at the Olderion inn (100 Gil). The city floods, Alluvion returns, and Archwitch Fina takes Fina's place.
  • Waterfall Way (rec. Lv 24) leads to the Altar. There's no boss, but Gilgamesh on 3F talks about the Genji Gloves. Loot: Devout's Armor, Guardian Sword, Aquatic Veil.
  • Town of Amore: Rain collapses, and the cure only works for a while. Loot: Morning Star, Aegis Robe. The shop sells no Tents.
  • Warden's Cape: Nichol raises a stone bridge out of the sea. Alluvion ambushes you in water bubbles, but there's no fight.
7Water Shrine · rec. Lv 26
  • Take the first boat to the switch that opens the way.
  • Lower crate room: hit the switches left, right, left to reveal a hidden chest with the R'lyeh Text.
  • Lower the water to reach the lone box. Build crate bridges to get the Calm Armlet, which you'll want for the boss.
  • Other loot: Scale Mail, Piercing Mace. 3 shards, 6 chests.
  • Boss (B2): Alluvion. Afterward Archwitch Fina leaves and Fina returns.
8Wrap-up: go back to Olderion, go after Arsha, then take the port to Zoldaad, which leads into Chapter 4.

Boss cheat sheet

Wave Lopros · Cave of Waters · Lv 20 · 21,000 HP · stagger 600
  • Weak to ICE. Comes with Azure and Land Snappers.
  • Devour eats one of your party members, and “lures in more prey” calls in more enemies. Stagger it to stop both.
  • Each time it eats something it gets a buff, and it heals itself with Cannibalize (2,000 HP). Keep your heals ready.
  • Ice users: Lasswell (Blizzard at Lv 1), or Noctis, Terra, Tronn or Victoria Visions.
  • Drop: Water Scroll I
Water Dragon · Old Town · Lv 21 · 24,000 HP · stagger 600
  • Weak to LIGHTNING. Water attacks do half damage, so don't use them.
  • Bubble Breath is its big attack (3 Water hits). Charge can paralyze. Octo-Strike hits random targets 8 times.
  • Lightning users: Sakura (Lv 3), or Noctis or Tronn Visions.
  • Drop: Water Scroll I
Leviathan · Lake Dorr · Lv 23 · 40,000 HP · stagger 1,000
  • Weak to LIGHTNING. Resists Water.
  • Kill the Orbs of Light first to weaken it. It gets enraged if the fight drags on, so keep up the pressure.
  • Tsunami and Killer Tsunami each hit 5 times. Staggering it stops them.
  • Constrict paralyzes, so a Ribbon helps.
  • Drop: Magicite Shard
Alluvion · Water Shrine B2 · Lv 26 · 48,000 HP · stagger 850
  • Weak to LIGHTNING. Immune to Water.
  • Prism cores absorbed! Each core it absorbs makes its special stronger. Keep your HP high.
  • The Divine Signet inflicts Charm and Berserk. Equip Monk's Prayer Beads (Charm), a Calm Armlet (Berserk) or a Ribbon, and bring Remedies.
  • Maelstrom hits the whole party. Graceful Deluge removes your Protect and Shell.
  • Drops: Azure Amulet, Water Scroll II

Combat quick rules for this chapter

  • Bring Lightning. Three of the four bosses are weak to it. Wave Lopros is the exception (use Ice).
  • Don't use Water magic. Every boss here resists or is immune to it.
  • Save your big hits for the stagger bar. Staggering a boss cancels its most dangerous attacks.
  • Bring status protection for Paralysis (Leviathan) and Charm/Berserk (Alluvion).
